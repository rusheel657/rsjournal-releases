# RSJournal privacy policy

RSJournal is a personal notes app built for one person's own use.

- **Your notes stay yours.** Notes are stored on your device and, if you turn on sync, in a folder called `SecondBrain` in your own Google Drive. They are not sent anywhere else.
- **Google Drive access** is used only to read and write the files in that folder, so your phone and computer have the same notes. The app has no server of its own and stores no copy of your data.
- **Nothing is collected.** There are no analytics, ads, tracking or accounts.
- **Turning it off:** use "Turn off sync" in the app's Settings, or remove RSJournal's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your notes stay in your Drive.
